<section class="story-section" aria-labelledby="learning">
  <h2 id="learning">How I trained it</h2>
  <p>
    I first saved the inputs and outputs of Qwen’s attention module. Qwen stayed frozen. Hyena
    learned from those pairs, with mean squared error measuring how far its output was from Qwen’s.
  </p>
  <DistillationTrainingCanvas />
  <p class="experiment-context">
    I trained on an <strong class="gpu-name">AMD Instinct MI300X</strong>, with Qwen loaded in BF16.
    The run used 512 Wikipedia sequences: 358 for training, 77 for validation, and 77 for testing.
  </p>
</section>

    <summary id="checking">Full replacement evaluation</summary>
